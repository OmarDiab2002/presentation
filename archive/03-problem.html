The problem

Life admin is made of deadlines. Nothing collects them.

Insurance, bills, school fees, the car licence, warranties, medical follow-ups. Each one has a date, and that date usually sits inside a document nobody opens twice.

What the problem is

Every household runs on official dates. They arrive as PDFs, letters, and emails, and then they are forgotten. Today people keep them in their head, or type them into an app by hand — which only works if they already know the date.

Who has it

Working adults, parents, and people living abroad. They are running six areas of life at the same time — health, home, car, money, family, and pets — and every area brings its own paperwork and its own dates.

Why it matters

A missed date is not a small mistake. An expired insurance policy is a legal and financial risk. A late bill adds a fee. A lapsed renewal can mean starting the whole application again from the beginning.
