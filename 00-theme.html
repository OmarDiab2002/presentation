04

Rules the deck holds itself to

🎯

One accent per slide

Coral marks the one thing that matters on the slide. The near-black pill is the single primary emphasis. Paint everything and nothing reads.

🌿

Space over decoration

No card-in-a-card, no borders where whitespace works, no gradient blobs. Generous margins are the design — 80px sides, 64px top.

🧾

Provenance is non-negotiable

Any AI-derived value shown on a slide renders with its citation chip — exactly as the app does. It's the product's #1 risk mitigation, so it's on screen.
